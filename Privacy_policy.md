# Privacy Policy for Zavaj

**Effective Date:** October 4, 2026  
**Company Name:** Turkish Teknoloji A.S.  
**App Name:** Zavaj  

Turkish Teknoloji A.S. ("we," "our," or "us") operates the **Zavaj** mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

By accessing or using Zavaj, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. 

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide a secure and effective matchmaking experience, we collect several types of information for various purposes:

* **Account & Profile Information:** When you register an account, we collect your name, email address, gender, date of birth, profile pictures, and biographical details or preferences required to create your matchmaking profile.
* **Location Data:** With your explicit permission, we collect precise or approximate location data to help you discover matches near you and provide location-based features within the app.
* **Communications:** We collect the content of messages, images, and interactions you send and receive through the app's messaging features to ensure user safety and facilitate communication between matches.
* **Device and Usage Information:** We automatically collect certain information when you access the app, including your mobile device type, unique device identifiers, operating system, app crash logs, and usage statistics to monitor performance and improve our features.
* **Third-Party Sign-In:** If you choose to register or log in via third-party services (such as Google or Apple), we collect authentication tokens and basic profile details permitted by those services.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Turkish Teknoloji A.S. uses the collected data for various purposes:
* To create, manage, and authenticate your user account.
* To provide, operate, and maintain the Zavaj matchmaking service.
* To match you with other users based on your preferences and location.
* To enable in-app messaging and secure communication between users.
* To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, impersonation, and safety violations.
* To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms and policies.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:
* **Service Providers:** We may employ third-party companies and individuals (such as cloud hosting, analytics providers, and customer support tools) to facilitate our Service, perform service-related tasks, or assist us in analyzing how our Service is used. These third parties are bound by strict confidentiality obligations.
* **Legal Requirements:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or a government agency).
* **Safety and Protection:** We may disclose information to protect the rights, property, or safety of Turkish Teknoloji A.S., our users, or the general public.

## 4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us with your personal data and implement commercially acceptable technical and organizational security measures (including encryption in transit and secure database storage) to protect it. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

* **Retention:** We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law.
* **Account Deletion:** You can delete your account and associated personal data at any time directly through the app settings or by contacting us at our support email. Once requested, your profile and personal data will be permanently removed from our active databases in accordance with our data cleanup cycles.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is strictly restricted to individuals who are **18 years of age or older**. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take steps to remove such information and terminate the account.
